## Chapter 1: The Crimson Hood

<!-- DIRECTOR: The scene opens in a cramped, steam-filled apartment. The lighting is high-contrast: harsh neon blues from outside clashing with the warm, flickering orange of a dying heater. The camera focuses on the textures of worn synthetic fabrics. -->

In the subterranean tiers of Neo-Paris, where the smog is thick enough to chew, lived a courier known only as "Red." She was a striking figure in the slums—not for her face, but for her signature gear: a high-collared, crimson-dyed tech-hood that shielded her face from the acid rain and the prying eyes of the optic-scanners. 

Her mother, a weary woman whose lungs were scarred by industrial soot, adored her. Her grandmother, a retired data-broker living in the high-security "Old Sector," had gifted her the hood—a piece of artisanal weave that served as both a fashion statement and a physical firewall against the city's surveillance.

One evening, her mother handed her a pressurized canister of nutrient-paste and a decrypted data-shard.

"Go, Red," her mother whispered, her voice rasping with a chronic cough. "The Old Sector is under a heavy lockdown, but I hear your grandmother’s neural-link is failing. Take this. It’s the only way to keep her consciousness stable."

## Chapter 2: The Predator in the Wired Woods

<!-- DIRECTOR: Transition to the "Wired Woods"—a sprawling, abandoned industrial zone filled with derelict server towers and rusted scaffolding. The soundscape is a mix of dripping water and the hum of illegal power lines. -->

Red moved through the industrial wastes, a labyrinth of shadows. It was here she encountered the "Wolf"—a high-level corporate mercenary and data-thief known for his predatory efficiency. He was lurking near a cluster of heavy-duty security drones, his presence masked by a sophisticated active-camouflage suit.

He couldn't strike yet; the drones were patrolling the perimeter, their red beams sweeping the wreckage.

"A courier?" the Wolf asked, his voice modulated through a vocal synthesizer to sound like a friendly, low-frequency hum. "Where are you heading, little bird?"

Red, trusting the civility of his synthetic tone, didn't realize she was interacting with a predator. "I’m heading to the Old Sector. I’m delivering a stability-shard to my grandmother's uplink. She’s... fading."

"Is it far?" the Wolf asked, his eyes glowing with a predatory amber light.

"Past the smelting plant, at the first hab-block on the edge of the High Sector," she replied.

"I know the way," the Wolf purred. "I’ll take the maintenance tunnels. You take the main thoroughfare. We shall see who reaches the terminal first."
