## Chapter 1: The Vertical Ascent

<!-- DIRECTOR: Establish a high-contrast, noir atmosphere. Constant rain, flickering neon, and a sense of suffocating industrial decay. -->

In the sprawling verticality of Sector 7, the "Hillside" was not a mountain, but the Apex—a luxury hydroponic zone where the elite consumed real, organic nutrients while the rest of the city choked on synthetic paste. Three data-miners, all bearing the street name "Gruff," were on a mission to breach the Apex and extract enough high-grade bio-matter to sell on the black market.

To reach the summit, they had to traverse the "Overpass," a rusted, swaying pedestrian bridge spanning a toxic drainage canal. Beneath the bridge lived a "Troll"—a massive, decommissioned security drone, its chassis warped and fused with illegal, twitching cybernetics. It had optic sensors the size of dinner plates and a jagged, elongated sensor-array for a nose.

<!-- THEME: Represents the disparity between the low-life (miners) and the high-tech (the Apex), while the Troll represents the predatory nature of obsolete, violent technology left to rot in the gutters. -->

<!-- IMAGE: A wide shot of the rain-slicked bridge, glowing with flickering blue and pink neon, suspended over a steaming green sludge. -->

First came the youngest Gruff, a scrawny kid with a cheap neural-link and a nervous twitch.

*Clang, hiss, clang, hiss.* went the bridge as his boots hit the metal.

"Who’s scanning my bridge?" the Troll roared, its speakers crackling with static-laced bass.

"It’s just me," the kid squeaked, his voice trembling through a low-quality vocoder. "I’m just a small-time runner heading up to the Apex to get my fill."

"I’m coming to fry your neural-path," the Troll growled, its servos whirring.

"Please, don't," the kid pleaded. "I’m too small a payday for you. Wait for the second Gruff. He’s carrying heavier hardware."

"Fine," the Troll spat, its red sensors dimming. "Get out of my sight."

A short while later, the second Gruff appeared. He was a mid-level enforcer, his body reinforced with industrial-grade pistons.

*Clang, hiss, clang, hiss, clang, hiss.* the bridge groaned under his weight.

"Who’s tramping over my bridge?" the Troll roared, its optics glowing brighter.

"It’s the second Gruff," the miner said, his voice steady but lacking the grit of a true veteran. "I’m heading to the Apex for the big haul."

"I’m coming to scrap you for parts," the Troll threatened.

"Don't waste your time on me," the second Gruff countered. "Wait for the big one. He’s got the heavy-duty rigs. He’s the real deal."

"Very well," the Troll hissed. "Move along."
