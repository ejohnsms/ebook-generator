## Chapter 1: The Path to the High Pastures

In the vibrant village of Port Coral, nestled between a turquoise lagoon and a lush jungle, lived three sturdy goats known locally as the "Gruff Brothers." They weren't ordinary goats; they were the pride of the village, known for their thick coats and even thicker tempers.

One scorching afternoon, the three brothers decided they needed to reach the High Pastures—a hidden plateau overflowing with the sweetest wild grass and succulent tropical shrubs. The goal was simple: eat until they were so round and prosperous that they’d be the envy of every farmer in the parish.

To get there, however, they had to cross the Old Stone Arch. This bridge spanned a roaring, limestone-filled river that fed into the sea. For generations, the villagers whispered of the "Shadow-Man," a creature of swamp and salt who lived beneath the arch. He was a hulking, moss-covered beast with eyes like glowing embers and a nose as long and crooked as a driftwood branch.

## Chapter 3: The Middle Brother’s Stand

A short while later, the middle brother, Middle Gruff, stepped onto the arch. He was broader in the chest and carried himself with more confidence.

*Thump, thump, thump.*

"Who dat a-thumpin' on my bridge?" the Shadow-Man bellowed, his voice echoing off the canyon walls.

"It’s me, the middle brother," he replied, his voice steady and clear. "I’m headed for the High Pastures to make myself fat for the feast."

The Shadow-Man lurched upward, his long nose twitching. "Now, I’m comin' to feast on your meat!"

"Hold your fire, Shadow-Man!" Middle Gruff shouted back. "Don't you go waste your appetite on me. I’m still a rung down the ladder. Wait for the eldest. He’s a mountain of a goat, much bigger than I be."

The Shadow-Man hissed, a sound like steam escaping a pot. "Big brother, you say? Very well. Be off with you!"
